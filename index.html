About Bright Links

Founded on a commitment to international trade integrity and modern technological capability, Bright Links operates specialized commercial entities across the Middle East, North America, Europe, and Asia. We combine local regulatory knowledge, direct manufacturer sourcing, and end-to-end execution to deliver dependable results for global clients and institutional partners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Bright Links, our worldwide entities, sourcing capabilities, and technology services.

What core services does Bright Links offer across its companies?

Each Bright Links entity delivers dedicated expertise: Dubai (trading, export, import, electronics, computers, hardware & gadgets), South Korea (electronics import & export from Korea to Dubai), United Kingdom & Portugal (software, IT, social media marketing, service providing & documentation consultancy), Pakistan (documentation, consultancy, business planning & business consultancy), and USA (e-commerce operations).

Where is your Global Headquarters located?

Our Global Headquarters is situated in Dubai, UAE at Office 45 Abdulrahman Al-Bahr Building 205, Al-Khabaisi, Deira, Dubai, operating under Head of Dubai Operations, Hafiz Muhammad Fiaz.

What services do your USA, UK, and Portugal companies provide?

Our USA (America) company focuses exclusively on modern E-Commerce. Our United Kingdom (+44 7440 508797) and Portugal (+351 920 266 093) companies specialize in software development, IT solutions, social media marketing, service providing, and documentation consultancy.
