Global Headquarters – Dubai, UAE

Serving as the international trading command center for Bright Links, specializing in trading, import, export, electronics, computers, hardware, and gadgets worldwide.

Head of Dubai Operations: Hafiz Muhammad Fiaz

Dubai is the cornerstone of Bright Links, operating as our primary hub for trading, export, and import of electronics, computers, hardware, and gadgets. From this strategic international trade center, we coordinate high-volume commercial distribution across the Middle East, Europe, Africa, Asia, and North America.

Backed by our direct electronics import-export pipeline from South Korea into Dubai, our product portfolio covers commercial computers, precision computer hardware, consumer electronics, smartphones, and modern gadgets, delivered with Dubai's world-class logistics speed and reliability.
